## Decision

A leaf records a call with stakes: a product call, or a technical call whose flip would change what the project's users get, cost real rework, or that the owner wants settled. A technical description of a system, and a technical choice an agent could flip without anyone outside the code noticing, go in a spec, even when a person could weigh them. The tripwire: would the owner want to be asked before this flips? If not, it is spec.

## Why

Leaves are what the owner ratifies and what agents never re-question. Every low-stakes leaf spends the owner's attention and freezes something agents should keep free to change, and enough of them bury the calls that matter.

## Consequences

- A non-trivial choice without stakes is still recorded, in the spec it changes; git keeps why it changed.
- The leaf's Why says what the stakes are; a Why that can name none is a sign the text belongs in a spec.
