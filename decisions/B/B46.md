## Decision

A leaf that states a MUST is cited by the test that enforces it, or its Consequences carry an `*Unenforced:*` line saying why no test can. A bug fix ships with the check that would have caught it.

## Why

Tests are the layer that enforces (A8), and a rule nothing checks is broken silently. A label keeps the debt visible where a reader of the rule will see it, instead of leaving the reader to assume the rule holds.

## Consequences

- `validate` warns on a MUST that is neither tested nor labeled; `must_test = off` turns the warning off.
- The leaf never names its tests; the test cites the leaf.
- Whether a citing test truly enforces its MUST is a reviewer's call, not the tool's.
- Triage of the labeled debt is the adopter's process (B57).
