## Decision

DTE supplies the format, the tool and the rules for writing and changing leaves. It defines no triage, backlog, release routine, house voice, naming habit for specs, or doc routine; an adopter sets those up its own way, and may record them as its own leaves.

## Why

A package that dictates process is one an existing project cannot drop in (A4). Every adopter already has habits around its work; DTE's job is the tree those habits point at.

## Consequences

- Where a DTE rule touches process (labeling an unenforced `MUST`), DTE defines the label and stops.
- What each ring means beyond A is the adopter's map, as is which ring a subagent gets.
