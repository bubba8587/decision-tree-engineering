## Decision

A leaf's Decision, Why and Consequences cite only shallower rings. History may name anything, since it records what was true then.

## Why

A leaf is weighed against its parents alone (A9, B28). A parent that leans on a decision made under it cannot be judged without its own children, and the order the rings give stops meaning anything.

## Consequences

- `validate` fails a `[[ID]]` or `dte:ID` in a leaf's body (History and Contest excepted) whose ring is deeper than the leaf's own.
- What a parent implies for the rings below is stated in its own terms; the child says how it follows.
