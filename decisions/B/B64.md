## Decision

When a part of a spec has to stop being fluid (a format others depend on, a number the owner chose, a constraint learned the hard way), that part becomes a leaf, and the spec cites the leaf at that point instead of restating it. A spec has no other way to freeze text: no "do not change" notes, no locked sections.

## Why

Agents change specs freely, and that is the point of a spec. Only a leaf carries ratification, protection from agents and a blast radius, so a freeze written anywhere else is a sentence the next agent can edit away.

## Consequences

- Freezing is the owner's call. An agent that thinks part of a spec should be frozen proposes the leaf through the inbox.
- Once ratified, the leaf is human-held, so changing the frozen part needs the owner's authorization.
- Unfreezing is retiring the leaf and putting its text back in the spec.
