## Decision

A leaf in ring A has no parents. A leaf in any other ring has at least one parent, and every parent is in a strictly shallower ring than the leaf. A parent may be any number of rings up; it need not be the ring immediately inside.

## Why

A2 needs every leaf to trace to the core, which forces at least one parent. A1 needs precedence to be a strict order, which forces parents to be strictly shallower: a leaf cannot outrank something it descends from. Allowing a parent several rings up keeps the tree honest when a detail descends straight from a core goal with nothing in between.

## Consequences

- `dte validate` errors on parentless non-core leaves and on parents at the same or deeper ring.
- A move that would violate this must re-parent or move children first (SPEC section 7).
