## Decision

Coverage counts an artifact as covered when it cites a leaf or is built to a spec that does. An artifact that cites nothing because no decision governs it is listed in `.dtecoverage` under a `why:` line saying so. Coverage reports three gauges, one per hop: artifacts to a leaf or spec, specs to a leaf, and MUSTs to a test.

## Why

The tree is meant to be total, so 100% has to be reachable and has to mean something. A file that needs no decision is a claim, and writing the claim down with its reason is what lets the rest be expected to cite. One blended number hid which hop was thin.

## Consequences

- A glob before any `why:` line is an error; a glob that matches nothing is stale.
- An excluded file is still scanned, and one that cites anyway counts.
- "Not swept yet" is not a reason.
- `coverage --check` exits 1 below 100% on the first two gauges, on a stale glob, or on an unlabeled `MUST`.
