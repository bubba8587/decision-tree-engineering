## Decision

The agent protocol DTE ships (`PROTOCOL.md`) carries only what every DTE repo needs; DTE's own rings and conventions live in its `CLAUDE.md`. `vendor` copies the protocol, spec, adoption guide, walkthrough and a render of DTE's decisions, and prints the A and B leaves added or retired since the last stamp.

## Why

DTE is adopted by other repos (A4). A protocol that carries this repo's rings or its Python conventions teaches an adopter's agents rules that are not theirs, and a refresh that says nothing leaves the adopter to diff rule text by hand.

## Consequences

- The stamp names the copy's own refresh command and nothing about the source checkout.
- `ADOPTING.md` has a "when the rules change" sweep for an existing tree.
