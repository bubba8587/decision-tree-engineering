## Decision

The node `title` is the short dense summary A5 requires. Every tool output
that names a node prints `ID title`. Validate warns when a title exceeds 100
characters. With `summaries = off` in `dte.cfg` the tool prints bare IDs.

## Why

A5 needs a summary string per node and a switch. Reusing `title` avoids two
fields drifting apart. 100 characters is roughly one sentence, long enough to
carry a decision and short enough to sit inside a chat line beside the ID.

## Consequences

- Node authors write titles as statements of the decision, not topic labels.
- The `off` switch exists for users who have the tree open beside the chat.
