## Decision

Before its first task that works under a leaf, a top-level agent asks the owner which ring it holds, unless someone already said. Until answered, and in a run nobody watches, it works at the shallowest ring that can still build, which the adopter names. Subagents are briefed one ring deeper than their spawner.

## Why

Authority follows ring (A6), and a ring nobody declared cannot be checked. Assuming a ring is how an agent ends up editing above it without noticing.

## Consequences

- `validate` warns when leaves changed with no ring declared (`--as` or `DTE_RING`); the owner sets `DTE_OWNER=1`.
- Which ring an unanswered agent takes is the adopter's to say, in its own instructions.
