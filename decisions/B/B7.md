## Decision

Every node carries `made_by` (`human`, `ai`, `joint`), `by` (free text naming
the person or model), and `date`. AI and joint nodes may be `active` without
a human's `ratified_by`, but validation lists every unratified one on every
run until a human fills the field.

## Why

A3 requires provenance to be captured at the moment of decision. Requiring
ratification before a node can be active would block agents mid-task and push
them toward not recording decisions at all, which is worse. Surfacing instead
of blocking keeps the record honest and keeps the owner's review queue
visible.

## Consequences

- `dte validate` always ends with the unratified list.
- Ratification is a one-field edit by a human (SPEC section 7).
