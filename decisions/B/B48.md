## Decision

A child records a different question that its parent leaves open. A leaf that restates part of its parent, adds detail to it, or names one aspect of it is not a child: it folds back into the parent's Consequences, or becomes spec.

## Why

The tree's shape is what makes blast radius and precedence computable (A9). An aspect filed as a child adds a leaf to every radius and a place for the parent's rule to be contradicted, without adding a decision anyone could take separately.

## Consequences

- `dte fold <child>` moves the child's Decision and Consequences into the parent, its citations and children with them, and writes a `folded` ledger line.
- One decision may govern several things when turning it off for one would break the others; those stay one leaf.
- Things that were ever reversed independently stay separate.
