## Decision

A leaf body holds what stands, why, and what it makes true. The Why is reasoning only: no dates, no account of who said what when, no adoption story. `## History` is one line per event (created, ratified, moved, reworded, contested) and nothing else. `## Contest` holds a contest's rubric, cases, costs and verdict until ratification. Ratifying drops both: History restarts at the ratification line, and git keeps everything before it. The owner may then rewrite the body in their own voice. `## Alternatives considered` is a terse list of the other ways it could have gone and stays.

## Why

A tree can have history but it cannot become a dev log; every agent that reads the leaf pays for the clutter in context, and a body full of settled argument hides the decision A2 wants findable. Before ratification the activity log helps the ratifier see what changed; after it, the ratified text is what stands and the record of getting there is git's.

## Consequences

- `dte ratify` drops Contest and History and writes one History line naming the drop.
- `validate` warns when a ratified leaf still carries a Contest section.
- `contested_by` stays, since it is provenance.
