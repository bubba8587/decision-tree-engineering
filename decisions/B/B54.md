## Decision

A branch that changes the product for its own purpose (built on the newest of everything, say) keeps an A leaf saying how, with the B leaves and specs under it, all living only on that branch. The root carries `branch: <name>` and the subtree inherits it. Findings move to other branches as ported changes, never by merging the subtree.

## Why

A specialized branch makes real decisions, so they belong in a tree (A9), but they bind only that branch. Marking the subtree lets the tree tell "lives elsewhere" from "deleted without a ledger line" (A1).

## Consequences

- `validate` warns when a branch-local root sits on another branch, and does not count its departure from one as a deletion.
- `next` still reserves the subtree's ids everywhere, so a port never collides.
