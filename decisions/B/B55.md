## Decision

A spec opens by citing what it serves. It states the rule, not who ruled or when; one paragraph carries one point; every sentence reads alone, naming its subject instead of "it" across sentences; and it never addresses the reader as "you".

## Why

Specs are read by builders who hold no ring and never open the tree (A8, B42), often one paragraph at a time, so a sentence that leans on the one before it is a sentence a builder misreads.

## Consequences

- History belongs to git, not to the spec.
- An adopter adds its own house style on top.
