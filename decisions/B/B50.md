## Decision

DTE calls one recorded decision a leaf: the vocabulary, the tool's output and the rule text all say leaf. The files that cite leaves are artifacts.

## Why

DTE is adopted into other projects (A4), and "node" collides with any product that has nodes of its own, so a sentence about the tree reads as one about the product. A leaf is unambiguous and fits the tree the rings already describe.

## Consequences

- The tool prints "leaves", `export` writes a `leaves` key, and `DTE.base` lists "All leaves".
- Code, config, docs and tests are artifacts.
