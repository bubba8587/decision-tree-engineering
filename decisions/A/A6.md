## Decision

Every agent operates at a ring. It may make decisions at its ring or deeper. Any decision that belongs shallower than its ring, and any decision whose ring is unclear, is not made by the agent: it is escalated to the agent that spawned it, or to the human, with the question "where does this belong?". The tool is built so that asking is the path of least resistance.

## Why

A new goal can arrive at any time, and without a rule the agent has no structure that makes it ask where the goal belongs.

This is also the interface between DTE and an agent hierarchy of the kind graph engineering describes: a high-level model at a shallow ring directs subagents to decide only within their deeper rings and to hand anything above that back up. Precedence (A1) makes the ring order meaningful; provenance (A3) makes it visible who decided; A6 makes the ring order the allocation of decision rights.

Authority is fluid on the human side. The owner may decide at any ring, all the way down. The structure constrains agents, not people (see B13).

## Consequences

- An unplaced decision has somewhere to live and a visible prompt to ask (B14, the inbox).
- Agents can declare their ring to the tool and have their changes checked against it (B15).
- An advisory authority map says who holds each ring, so an agent knows whom to ask (B13, B12).
- Overriding a human-held decision is a special case of acting above your authority and is protected separately (B11).

## Contest

### Contest 2026-10-31 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Rubric: the project's purpose as the owner stated it (A1 blastRadius, A3 provenance, A7 autonomy).
- keep: agents decide at their ring or deeper and escalate the rest. Cost: none new; the inbox, `--as` and the brief already carry it.
- opposite: any agent decides at any ring. Cost: A1's precedence stops meaning anything once a subagent can write ring A; the owner loses the only structural guarantee about who decided what.
- deletion: no authority rule; agents use judgment. Cost: A7 autonomy then has no boundary, and every agent re-asks or oversteps, the two failures DTE exists to remove.
- variant: authority by subtree instead of ring. Cost: needs a second hierarchy beside the rings; Solenoid ran on rings alone for a month without wanting it.
Verdict: keep. Rings are the one ordering DTE has, and A6 is what makes them an allocation of decision rights.
