## Decision

DTE must be applicable to an existing, live project without a rewrite, and it
works alongside structural methods such as graph engineering rather than
replacing them. The first external target is Solenoid NGC.

## Why

The owner set two constraints in the founding description: DTE "works
alongside, rather than supersedes, something like graph engineering", and this
repo is to be built "such that it can be applied to my main project, Solenoid
NGC". Both are constraints on *everything* below, which is what makes them
core: any format or tool choice that would only work in a greenfield repo, or
that tried to absorb dependency tracking, would violate them.

## Consequences

- Plain-text files and a single-file, dependency-free tool, so DTE can be
  dropped into any stack.
- Incremental coverage rather than all-or-nothing validation.
- DTE models decisions and precedence only. Structural relationships stay in
  the structural tool, and the two cross-reference (see B9).
