## Decision

DTE organizes a project into three layers: decisions, specs and implementation. The decision tree holds why: goals, rules, choices and their provenance. Specs hold what: what to build, generated from the tree and citing the leaves they serve; a choice a spec forces (a dependency, a format) is recorded as a deeper leaf first. Code and tests hold how, and cite the leaves the spec told them to. A layer cites upward and never stores anything from below: a leaf never lists its tests, specs or files; those lists are derived from citations at read time.

## Why

Writing "Enforced by: test" lines into leaves is a breach of scope: authored pointers downward go stale, and they pull test data into a document a human must keep readable. A test that cites the leaf is the same fact, kept where it can be checked. The same boundary answers the spec-tooling question: Spec Kit, Kiro and OpenSpec live in the middle layer, and DTE is the rationale layer under them, never a fourth spec format.

## Consequences

- dte.cfg gains `specs` and `tests` globs beside `docs`; `show` prints "specified by", "implemented by" and "enforced by" as derived lists; `coverage` reports in-effect leaves with no citing test. No `enforced_by` field.
- `dte spec <ID>` emits a spec skeleton from a leaf and its subtree for a tree agent to fill.
- Reverting a leaf's blast radius includes its specs and everything built to them.

## Contest

### Contest 2026-10-31 by Claude Opus 5.5: keep wins

Rubric: the owner's purpose (A1 blastRadius, A2 nothingUndecided, A4 dteIsPackage).
- keep: decisions hold why, specs hold what, code holds how; each cites upward and stores nothing from below. Cost: none; Solenoid's coverage reached 100% on exactly this split, with covers: globs carrying class-wide rules down.
- opposite: leaves list their specs, tests and files. Cost: every list goes stale the day code moves; A1's radius becomes a stored guess instead of a computed fact.
- deletion: a tree with no spec layer. Cost: mechanics pile into leaves; Solenoid found a third of its leaves were mechanics once the line was drawn, and the tree stopped being readable by a person.
- variant: two layers, leaves and code, with specs as optional docs. Cost: loses the builder role (B42) and the covers: scoping; no saving.
Verdict: keep.
