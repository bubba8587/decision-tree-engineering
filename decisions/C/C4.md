## Decision

`dte coverage` lists every scanned artifact with no citation and prints the
percentage covered. Missing coverage never fails validation.

## Why

A4 requires adoption into a live project. On day one in Solenoid NGC coverage
will be near zero; failing validation on that would make the tool unusable
there. Coverage as a visible number that only goes up is the adoption
strategy: pick a subsystem, write its ring, cite it, watch the number move.

## Consequences

- Structural errors (unknown IDs, orphans, bad parents) still fail
  validation; only *absence* of citations is tolerated.
- Teams may choose to gate on a coverage floor in CI; DTE does not.
