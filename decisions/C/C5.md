## Decision

A leaf with `status: proposed` counts as in effect for precedence, blast radius, and parenting. Any in-effect leaf whose parent is proposed produces a validation warning, not an error.

## Why

B7 lets AI leaves exist before ratification; the same reasoning applies to core goals the AI drafts from the owner's words (A2 to A4). Work has to be able to descend from a proposed goal or nothing could be built until every core leaf was ratified. The warning keeps the unratified foundation visible so it does not become permanent by neglect.

Held with medium confidence: the owner may prefer that nothing descends from a proposed core leaf at all.
