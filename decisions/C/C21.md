## Decision

`dte authorize` writes `authorized_by` on nodes that already exist and appends a History line with an optional note. It is the command that owns B11's field; `set` still refuses it.

## Why

An owner who says go ahead after a node exists needs a way to be recorded, or `validate --as` fails on every ring-A draft the owner asked for. B29 puts each invariant-bearing field behind its own command.

## Consequences

- ADOPTING's bootstrap: the agent drafts ring A as ai unratified, runs authorize with the owner's name, the owner ratifies later.
