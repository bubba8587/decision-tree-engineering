## Decision

`validate` prints errors and warnings in full, then one line of counts for unratified (with contested and imported), then names each unratified leaf of rings A and B with how many leaves sit below it, since everything under them inherits an unsettled rule; then the pending, outbox and scope counts and the changed-leaves block. `validate --full` and `dte unratified` print the whole unratified list, shallowest ring first and widest first within a ring. The per-leaf no-children-no-citations warning is gone from validate; `scope` reports it as no reach.

## Why

At 150 leaves a full list makes validate print 300 lines per run and the OK scrolls away; on day one every A leaf warns. B7 surfaces unratified leaves; a count surfaces them without filling an agent's context. B17 needs the changed-leaves block kept.
