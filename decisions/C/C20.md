## Decision

`new --body-file FILE` uses the file's sections as the body (frontmatter stripped, Decision and Why required), beating the section flags. `dte import DIR --by WHO` reads every .md in DIR: frontmatter `ring`, `title`, optional `name` and `parents` (ids, names, or other files by name), any known node property; allocates ids in dependency order; writes the History line B33 requires; rolls back on the first error.

## Why

B33 demands properties carried unchanged and a parent-first allocation; B29 demands the tool write every frontmatter. Shell quoting on Windows mangles multi-paragraph bodies passed as arguments.

## Consequences

- Source files are left in place; the tool says to remove the folder when done.
