## Decision

When `retire --superseded-by` carries a contest record into the successor, it also sets the successor's `contested_by` and logs it, so the node that was the contest's answer is settled. `contest --record` with a non-keep verdict and `--title` (plus a body) writes the winner at the same ring with the same parents, `contested_by` set, and retires the loser superseded by it; `--chosen deletion --retire` reverts in the same run.

## Why

FEEDBACK.md K3: the winner of a contest was listed as uncontested and asked for a second contest. B28 says a contested node is settled; the settlement must travel with the text. The three-command gap between verdict, new and retire is where the bug lived.
